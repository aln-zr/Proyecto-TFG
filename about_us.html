About the Band

Our music is a journey through the chaos of emotion and the weight of the human experience. We merge crushing riffs with haunting melodies, weaving together electronic textures and heavy instrumentation to create an experience, something visceral.


Our songs explore the duality of beauty and brutality, the tension between melody and aggression. Every lyric, every beat is crafted to pull you deeper into the atmosphere, blurring the line between the ethereal and the heavy. We are more than a band. We are a movement, a voice for those lost in the static—those searching for meaning in the echoes of a world that never stops screaming.
